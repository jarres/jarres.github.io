Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

meeting-pipeline is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads and writes files in the owner's Google Drive, limited in practice to the folders it uses for recordings and transcripts.

How the data is used

Recordings are processed only to produce transcripts and related notes for the owner. Results are saved back to the owner's Google Drive.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Processing runs on cloud computing resources controlled by the owner.

Retention and removal

Files stay in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
